Emergency help

The first 10 minutes matter more than the next 10 days

Water does not sit still. It spreads across floors, wicks up drywall, and soaks into padding and subfloors. In Hollywood's humidity, a wet wall that waits a day can take a week to dry — and the longer materials stay wet, the more of the job turns into repair.

  • Stop the source if it is safe. Shut the main valve, or turn off the feeding appliance.
  • Move what matters. Lift rugs, electronics and papers out of the water path.
  • Take photos before you move anything. Your insurer will want to see it as found.
  • Call (754) 294-7959. Say where the water is, whether it is still flowing, and how far it spread.
  • Leave the big equipment to the crew. Household fans cannot pull water out of a wall cavity or a subfloor.

The first job is water damage cleanup: get the water out before it moves into places you cannot see. Mapping, drying and verification make sure the cleanup actually finished the job. A wall left wet for a weekend can cost a room. See our guide to what to do after water damage.

Causes

What floods Hollywood homes — and what it takes to fix it

Most calls in this city come from the same six sources. Clean water from a supply line, gray water from an appliance, and black water from a sewer backup are three different jobs, with different safety rules and drying times.

💥

Burst pipes & plumbing failures

Supply lines and aging fittings release clean water under pressure. Shut the valve, then call — a pipe that burst at 2 a.m. can wet three rooms by sunrise.

🔧

Appliance leaks

Washers, dishwashers, water heaters and ice-maker lines leak slowly behind the unit, so the damage is found late — warped flooring, a damp wall.

🏠

Roof & ceiling leaks

Storm water travels along decking before it appears indoors, sometimes rooms away from the penetration.

🌧️

Storms & flooding

Heavy rain and wind-driven water. Ground floors and garages take the first water, and it keeps coming until the rain stops.

🚽

Sewage & drain backups

Category 3 black water carries real health risk. It needs containment and sanitizing — never a mop and household cleaner.

❄️

AC & condensate overflows

AC runs most of the year here, and a clogged condensate line quietly wets ceilings and closet floors for weeks.

That is what water damage restoration Hollywood, FL homeowners actually need: the right response for the source. The industry word for this first phase is water remediation — the emergency work that stops the spread and keeps a contained problem from becoming a whole-house repair. For a slab leak under the floor or a leak from above, see slab leak cleanup and ceiling leak cleanup.

Process

The water damage restoration process, step by step

Restoration is a sequence, not a single visit.

  1. Call and safety check. We confirm the address, whether water is still flowing, and any electrical or contamination hazards.
  2. Stop the source. The leak is addressed first. Drying a space with active water entry wastes the effort.
  3. Water extraction. Standing water comes out with professional extraction equipment.
  4. Moisture mapping. Meters and thermal imaging locate damp areas inside walls, under flooring and in cavities. See moisture mapping.
  5. Drying and monitoring. Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings support a dry standard — not until the surface looks dry. See structural drying.
  6. Cleaning, sanitizing and documentation. Affected surfaces are cleaned as needed, and you receive photos, readings and a summary for your insurer. See insurance documentation.

Drying time

How long does drying take?

Timelines depend on the water category, how long materials stayed wet, and what was affected.

  • Emergency response and extraction: same day, usually within hours.
  • Structural drying: typically 3 to 5 days with professional equipment.
  • Repairs and rebuilding: 1 to 3 weeks for drywall, flooring and finishes.

A small clean-water leak on tile dries far faster than a Category 3 flood across several rooms. A water damage restoration service in Hollywood, FL should tell you, in numbers, when a wall is dry — not by looking at it, but by reading it. Read why structural drying matters and how fast mold grows.

Cost

Water damage restoration cost: what decides the number

No honest price exists before someone sees the job. Cost follows five things — not the size of the puddle.

  • Water category. Clean, gray and black water are different jobs. Black water adds containment and sanitizing. Read the Category 1, 2 and 3 guide.
  • Area affected. One bathroom is one job; water through three rooms is another.
  • How long it stayed wet. Hours are a drying job. Days add material replacement.
  • What got wet. Tile and concrete dry fast. Drywall, hardwood and padding hold water and often need partial replacement.
  • Drying days and equipment. More machines for more days is the largest variable on most invoices.

FAQ

Questions Hollywood homeowners ask

How much does water damage restoration cost in Hollywood, FL?

There is no fixed price until someone sees the job. Cost depends on the water category, how much area is wet, how long materials stayed wet, what the materials are, and how many drying days the job needs. A small clean-water leak on tile costs far less than a Category 3 flood across several rooms.

How long does the water damage restoration process take?

Extraction usually happens the same day you call. Structural drying typically takes 3 to 5 days with professional equipment. Repairs after drying — drywall, flooring, paint — usually take 1 to 3 weeks depending on scope. Dirty water, dense materials and high humidity can stretch every stage.

What should I do first when I find water damage?

Stop the source if you can do it safely — shut the main water valve for a plumbing leak, or move valuables out of the water path. Stay out of standing water near outlets and appliances. Take photos of everything before you move it, then call (754) 294-7959. Do not run a household vacuum on standing water.

Will my homeowners insurance cover water damage restoration?

Many sudden and accidental losses are covered under a standard homeowners policy, while flooding from external rising water is usually a separate policy. We do not decide coverage — your carrier does. What we provide is the documentation your adjuster asks for: photos, moisture readings, equipment logs and a written scope of work.

Can I stay in my home while it dries?

Usually yes for a small clean-water loss in one area. For a Category 3 sewage backup, a whole-home flood, or rooms where drying equipment fills the living space, staying elsewhere for a few days is often the practical choice. The equipment is loud and runs around the clock, so light sleepers should plan for that.

How do I know it is really dry — and will mold grow?

Dry is a reading, not a look. Moisture meters measure the wall, floor and subfloor against a dry standard, and equipment stays in place until those readings hold. Mold can start growing on wet drywall and paper within 24 to 48 hours, which is why fast extraction and verified drying matter more than anything after a leak.

What is the fastest water damage fix for a small leak?

Stop the source, get the standing water out, and dry the area with airflow — then watch it for a week. A stain that keeps coming back means the leak is still active behind the surface.
