Frequently Asked Questions — Mapleridge

Our street was built all at once — should I worry about my pipes?

It is worth knowing where your main shutoff is and watching for warning signs like low pressure or discolored water. If a failure happens, fast extraction limits damage.

Can a broken sprinkler line really affect my house?

Yes — a ruptured irrigation main can pool water against the foundation and wick into wall bases. We extract, dry, and map the affected assemblies.

How do I find my main water shutoff?

Usually near the street meter or where the line enters the house. Find it before an emergency — it is the fastest way to stop a supply-side loss.

Will drying damage my floors?

No — our equipment is designed for building materials. We monitor to avoid over-drying and verify with readings.
