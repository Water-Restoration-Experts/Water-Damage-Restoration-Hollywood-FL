Frequently Asked Questions — Park East

Can stormwater from the park really reach my house?

In heavy rain, sheet flow can overwhelm drainage and reach low-lying properties. We check entry points and dry affected assemblies completely.

Do I need better drainage?

That is a landscaper or drainage contractor question — our role is extracting, drying, and documenting the current loss, plus noting observed entry points.

What about my garage?

Garages flood first and dry last. We extract, check wall bases and stored contents, and dry the structure.

Are tree roots a risk to my sewer line?

Mature trees near laterals can cause backups. Slow drains across fixtures are the warning sign — get a camera inspection from a plumber.
