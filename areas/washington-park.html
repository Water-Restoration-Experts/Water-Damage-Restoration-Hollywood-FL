Frequently Asked Questions — Washington Park

Are older pipes more likely to burst?

Age increases risk, especially with corrosion or past repairs. Warning signs include discolored water, low pressure, and recurring small leaks.

What about my old drain lines?

Aging drains can back up from root intrusion or buildup. We handle the resulting water damage; a plumber should inspect the line itself.

Can you dry older wall materials?

We assess each material and dry what can be saved, removing only what cannot be restored. Our meters guide those decisions.

Should I replace my water heater preventatively?

Heaters past their expected life are a leading cause of sudden floods. A plumber can advise on replacement; we handle it if one fails.
